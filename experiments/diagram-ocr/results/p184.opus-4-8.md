# claude-opus-4-8 vision pass — p184 (Fig 10.1 Keyboard Matrix)

Read from a 500dpi center-crop (`renders/p184-crop2.png`), the hardest figure
in the set: hand-lettered column/row labels and dual-character key cells.

## Key structural finding

From PA3 down, each cell is a **two-glyph key**: the shifted symbol printed
*above* the normal symbol (e.g. `!` over `1`). Both sonnet and glm-ocr
collapsed these to a single character and so dropped half the key legends.
Any faithful transcription must preserve both glyphs per cell.

## 1. Faithful transcription

- Column header (hand-lettered): **"Keyboard Data Port"**, columns `KD0`…`KD7`.
- Row labels down the right (hand-lettered "Key Strobe Port"): `PA0`…`PA7`, then `PB0`.

| Strobe | KD0 | KD1 | KD2 | KD3 | KD4 | KD5 | KD6 | KD7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PA0 | Z | X | C | V | B | N | M | L |
| PA1 | A | S | D | F | G | H | J | K |
| PA2 | Q | W | E | R | T | Y | U | I |
| PA3 | O | P | @ | `[` | `< ,` | `> .` | `? /` | `} ]` |
| PA4 | `! 1` | `" 2` | `# 3` | `$ 4` | `% 5` | `& 6` | `' 7` | `( 8` |
| PA5 | `) 9` | `= 0` (?) | `+ ;` (?) | `* :` | `= -` (?) | `\| ^` (?) | SPACE | PASTE/INS |
| PA6 | DEL/BS | ↑ | ↓ | ← | → | TAB | ESC | ⏎ (return) |
| PA7 | f·1 | f·2 | f·3 | f·4 | f·5 | — | — | STOP |
| PB0 | SHIFT | CTRL | GRPH | — | CAPS | — | — | — |

Dual-glyph cells are written `shifted normal`. `(?)` marks cells whose shifted
glyph I'm <90% sure of (PA5 is the worst row — small hand-lettering, faint scan).

## 2. Notes for Tier B

- PA3–PA5 need a human eye on the original (or a known PC-8201A keymap) to
  confirm the shifted symbols. The unshifted column is reliable.
- PA7 are the five function keys (printed `f·1`…`f·5`) plus STOP at KD7.
- This figure is **not** a clean win for any single model — the right move is
  sonnet (or opus) for the bulk + a human pass on PA3–PA5, cross-checked
  against the other model's reading to flag disagreements.
